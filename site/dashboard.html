Guardrails & fairness

Quality, harm, complaints, and parity are hard launch and runtime constraints—not optional annotations.

enforced Quality floor

Each experiment defines a minimum aggregate quality score. A breach terminates a running treatment.

enforced Harm & complaint ceilings

Normalized harm and complaint rates have explicit maximums, validated before launch and during evaluation.

enforced Fairness ratio

Opaque aggregate groups must each meet n ≥ 50. The worst-to-best positive-rate ratio cannot fall below 0.80.

prohibited Sensitive scoring inputs

Protected and sensitive keys are rejected recursively from policy features, context, candidate features, and metadata.

capped Exploration

Absolute exploration is capped at 10%. More than 3% makes a launch high risk and requires human approval.

prohibited Manipulative objectives

Compulsion, outrage, urgency, deceptive scarcity, and time-spent optimization terms fail policy validation.
