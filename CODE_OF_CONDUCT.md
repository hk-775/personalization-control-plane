# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, accessible, and harassment-free community.
Contributors should:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- give specific, constructive feedback;
- respect privacy, consent, and safety concerns;
- disclose conflicts of interest that could affect technical or ethical review;
- avoid publishing personal, confidential, or security-sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository
host's private contact or security channel. Maintainers may remove content,
limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
